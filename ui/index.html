SIH Project: WiFi CSI Architecture

How WiFi Channel State Information enables 100% privacy-preserving human sensing.

Why CSI Over Cameras?

Cameras cannot be placed in hostel bedrooms, bathrooms, or shower areas due to fundamental privacy violations. WiFi signals penetrate doors, thin walls, and steam, while CSI (Channel State Information) captures only raw physical signal attenuation and phase shifts, ensuring zero photographic recording of students.

Physics & Signal Processing

When a human body breathes, steps, or falls, it perturbs the 52 OFDM subcarriers of a standard 802.11n Wi-Fi frame. By computing the variance, Doppler spectrum, and band power across subcarriers, our micro-Doppler engine separates human motion from environmental ceiling fans and cooler noise.
